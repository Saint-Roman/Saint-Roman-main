The Saint Roman story

Born in the middle of a daily compromise

Saint Roman began with a simple observation: while outer fashion has evolved, the foundational layer closest to our skin remained uninspired, uncomfortable, and overlooked. We set out to change that from the thread up.

Identified the gap in the intimates market for high-performance, mass-premium Micro-Modal essentials engineered for Indian weather and active routines.

Textile Innovation

Digital Blitz & Scaling
