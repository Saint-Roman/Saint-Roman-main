At Saint Roman, we are committed to delivering precision-engineered, mass-premium intimates, loungewear, and daily essentials. Because our products are worn directly against the skin, our Return and Exchange Policy is crafted to balance customer satisfaction with strict health, hygiene, and public safety standards under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

1. Hygiene & health exclusions (non-returnable items)

Due to strict sanitary regulations and hygiene guidelines, intimate apparel categories cannot be returned or exchanged once the sealed packaging is opened or used.

Strictly Non-Returnable / Non-Exchangeable Categories:

  • Men's Innerwear Bottoms: Trunks, Briefs, Boxer Briefs, and Woven Boxers.
  • Women's Panties & Innerwear Bottoms: Hipsters, Bikinis, Thongs, Boyshorts, and Seamless Laser-Cut Briefs.
  • Kids' Underwear: Boys' Trunks/Briefs and Girls' Knickers/Briefs.
  • Opened Multi-Pack Boxes: Any 2-pack, 3-pack, or 5-pack box where the primary hygienic seal or individual product pouch has been opened or tampered with.

Exceptions: The only exceptions to this rule are verified manufacturing defects, transit damage, or dispatch of an incorrect size/item by Saint Roman.

2. Eligible categories for return or exchange

The following product lines are eligible for a 7-Day Return or Exchange Window from the date of delivery:

  • Women's Bras & Bralettes: Everyday T-Shirt Bras, Underwired Bras, Bralettes, and Sports Bras (must be unworn, with original tags intact and no deodorant/perfume marks).
  • Men's, Women's & Kids' Loungewear: Pyjama Sets, Lounge Shorts, Sleep Shirts, Kaftans, and Robes.
  • Kids' Outerwear & Thermals: Thermal Base-Layer Sets and Nightwear Sets.
  • Unopened Multi-Packs: Multi-pack boxes with their primary factory hygienic seals completely intact.

3. Mandatory return conditions

To qualify for a successful return or exchange, eligible items must satisfy all of the following requirements:

  • Tag & Packaging Integrity: Items must be in their original, unwashed, unworn, and unaltered condition, with all brand tags, care labels, hygiene strips, and magnetic packaging intact.
  • No Wear Marks: Items must be free of stains, makeup, body lotion, deodorant marks, perfume scents, or pet hair.
  • Proof of Purchase: The tax invoice, digital order confirmation, and original box must be returned along with the product.

4. Defective, damaged, or incorrect items

If you receive a defective garment, damaged packaging, or an incorrect size/item:

  • You must notify our Customer Support team within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Unboxing Video Mandate: To process claims for missing items, wrong sizes, or transit damage in intimate categories, we strongly request a clear, unedited unboxing video showing the sealed package being opened.
  • Upon verification, Saint Roman will arrange a free reverse pickup and issue a 100% free replacement or full refund without any additional processing fee.

5. Return & exchange procedure

  • Initiate a Request: Visit the Returns & Exchanges portal on www.saintromanfashion.com or open the Saint Roman App. Enter your Order ID and Registered Mobile Number.
  • Quality Check at Pickup: Our 3PL courier partner will initiate reverse pickup within 2–4 business days. The pickup agent will perform a preliminary physical check of the outer packaging and tags.
  • Facility Inspection: Once received at our central warehouse, our Quality Assurance (QA) team will inspect the item within 48 hours.
  • Approval / Rejection: If the item passes inspection, your exchange or refund will be authorized immediately. If an item fails inspection (e.g., shows signs of wear, washing, or missing tags), it will be returned to you at your expense.

6. Refund & store credit policy

  • Prepaid Orders (UPI, Cards, Net Banking): Approved refunds will be credited back to the original source payment account within 5–7 business days of QA approval.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Refunds for COD orders will be processed via Bank Transfer (NEFT/IMPS) or issued as an instant Saint Roman Store Credit/Gift Voucher, based on your preference. Customers must enter valid bank details on the returns portal.
  • Shipping & COD Charges: Original shipping fees and COD handling fees are non-refundable.

7. Cancellation policy

  • Before Dispatch: Orders can be canceled directly via the App or Website within 2 hours of placement or until the order status changes to "Dispatched." A 100% instant refund will be issued for prepaid orders.
  • After Dispatch: Once an order has been dispatched from our fulfillment center, it cannot be canceled. You may refuse delivery when the courier partner arrives, and a refund will be initiated after the shipment returns to our facility (minus applicable RTO freight charges).
