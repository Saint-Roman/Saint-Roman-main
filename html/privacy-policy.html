At ANNORIA HOUSE OF BRANDS Private Limited ("Saint Roman", "Company", "We", "Us", or "Our"), we are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our users, customers, and website/app visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, share, and protect your digital personal data when you interact with www.saintromanfashion.com, the Saint Roman Mobile Application, our social media channels, and our authorized physical retail touchpoints.

1. Statutory declaration & consent framework

  • In accordance with Section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), this Privacy Policy serves as a notice prior to or at the time of collecting your digital personal data.
  • By accessing our Services, placing an order, or clicking "I Agree / Accept," you grant unambiguous, free, specific, informed, and explicit consent to Saint Roman (acting as a "Data Fiduciary") to process your personal data solely for the lawful purposes defined in this policy.
  • Unbundled Marketing Consent: Consent for transactional order fulfillment (shipping, payment, delivery tracking) is managed independently from promotional marketing. You may complete purchases without opting into promotional campaigns.

2. Categories of data we collect

We collect only the personal data that is necessary to fulfill our commitments to you:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address, Billing Address, Shipping/Delivery Address, and PIN Code.
  • Transaction & Payment Data: Payment status, payment gateway transaction IDs, order history, invoice details, and refund preferences. (We do NOT store your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, or net-banking credentials.)
  • Technical & Device Data: IP Address, Device ID, Browser Type, Operating System, App performance logs, and Cookie identifiers.
  • Customer Support Data: Call recordings, chat transcripts, email communications, and sizing feedback provided to customer support.
  • Offline Retail Data: Name and mobile number provided at physical retail checkout counters for digital billing or loyalty programs.

3. Purpose of data processing

Your personal data is collected and processed exclusively for the following specified purposes:

Data Category Specific Purpose
Order Processing Fulfilling orders, processing payments, printing shipping labels, and dispatching multi-packs via 3PL partners.
Account & Support Managing your registered account, providing order tracking updates, and resolving sizing/return queries.
Service Improvement Analyzing app crashes, improving checkout UX, and optimizing inventory allocations based on aggregate demand.
Marketing (Opt-In) Sending personalized app push notifications, SMS/WhatsApp updates, and emails regarding new drops, only if you explicitly opt-in.
Legal Compliance Tax compliance (GST invoices), fraud prevention, and responding to lawful statutory mandates.

4. Data sharing and third-party processors

Saint Roman does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We share your data strictly on a need-to-know basis with trusted "Data Processors" bound by non-disclosure agreements and security obligations:

  • Logistics & Delivery Partners: (e.g., Delhivery, Shiprocket, BlueDart) to deliver your purchases.
  • Payment Gateways: (e.g., Razorpay, Cashfree, Paytm) to process secure, PCI-DSS compliant transactions.
  • Technology & Analytics Providers: (e.g., Firebase, Mixpanel, AWS) to host web/app infrastructure and analyze platform speed.
  • Communication Partners: (e.g., WhatsApp API, SMS Gateway providers) to send transactional OTPs and shipping tracking links.
  • Legal Authorities: When mandated by law, court order, or regulatory bodies under Indian jurisdiction.

5. Data storage, security, and retention

  • Security Safeguards: We implement technical, organizational, and physical security measures — including SSL encryption, end-to-end data transit security, and restricted server access — to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
  • Retention Period: We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or to comply with statutory accounting and legal retention obligations (e.g., maintaining tax logs under GST laws). Once the purpose is fulfilled or consent is withdrawn, your data is securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Your rights as a data principal

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you hold the following statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Summary: Request a summary of your personal data processed by Saint Roman and the identities of third parties with whom it has been shared.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request the correction of inaccurate or outdated data, or the deletion/erasure of personal data no longer required for its original purpose.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for data processing at any time through your account settings or by emailing our Grievance Officer. (Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing done prior to withdrawal.)
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate an individual who shall, in the event of death or incapacity, exercise your data protection rights.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Seek prompt resolution of any complaints regarding your personal data processing directly through our internal compliance channel.

7. Children's data privacy

  • Saint Roman offers kids' daily essentials (Ages 2–14); however, we do not directly process personal data or knowingly track minors under 18 years of age without verifiable parental or legal guardian consent.
  • All purchases and account creations for kids' ranges must be executed by a parent or legal guardian.

8. Cookie policy

We use essential, functional, and analytical cookies on our Website and App to ensure smooth navigation, remember shopping cart items, and analyze traffic patterns. You can manage or disable non-essential cookies through your web browser settings.

9. Grievance redressal officer & contact information

In accordance with the DPDP Act, 2023, and the IT Rules, 2021, if you wish to exercise your rights, request data deletion, or file a complaint regarding your privacy, please contact our designated Grievance Officer:

  • Title: Data Protection & Grievance Officer

10. Updates to this policy

We reserve the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational practices or legal requirements. Updated versions will be published on our digital channels with a revised "Last Updated" date.
