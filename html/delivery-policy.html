At Saint Roman, we are dedicated to delivering your everyday essentials swiftly, safely, and discreetly. This Shipping & Delivery Policy outlines our fulfillment processes, estimated timelines, logistics operations, and shipping charges in strict compliance with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

1. Coverage & logistics partners

Saint Roman delivers across 27,000+ PIN codes throughout India. We partner with premier third-party logistics (3PL) aggregators and premium courier carriers — including Delhivery, Blue Dart, Shiprocket, and Expressbees — to ensure end-to-end tracking and reliable delivery.

2. Processing & dispatch timelines

  • Order Processing SLA: All orders are processed at our central fulfillment center within 24 to 48 business hours of placement.
  • Operating Days: Dispatches occur Monday through Saturday, excluding Sundays and National Public Holidays.
  • Order Cut-Off: Orders placed before 1:00 PM IST on a working day are prioritized for same-day or next-day fulfillment.

3. Estimated delivery timelines

Once your order is handed over to our courier partner, the estimated delivery timelines are as follows:

Destination / Region Estimated Delivery SLA
Tier 1 & Metro Cities (e.g., Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata) 2 to 4 Business Days
Tier 2 & State Capitals (e.g., Pune, Jaipur, Ahmedabad, Lucknow, Chandigarh) 3 to 5 Business Days
Tier 3 & Rest of India 5 to 7 Business Days
Special / Remote Regions (e.g., North-East, Jammu & Kashmir, Island Territories) 7 to 10 Business Days

Note: Delivery times are indicative and may vary due to unforeseen weather conditions, regional lockdowns, flight disruptions, or festival operational surges.

4. Shipping rates & charges

Saint Roman maintains transparent pricing with zero hidden delivery fees at checkout:

  • Prepaid Orders Above ₹999: FREE Express Shipping nationwide.
  • Prepaid Orders Below ₹999: A nominal flat shipping charge of ₹49 is applied at checkout.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: A non-refundable COD Handling Fee of ₹49 applies to all COD orders to cover payment processing fees, regardless of cart value.

5. Order tracking & notifications

  • Dispatch Alert: As soon as your shipment leaves our warehouse, an automated notification containing your AWB (Airway Bill) Number and direct tracking link will be sent via SMS, WhatsApp, and Email.
  • Live Tracking: You can monitor your shipment's real-time journey by visiting the Track My Order section on www.saintromanfashion.com or within the Saint Roman Mobile App.

6. Discreet & sustainable packaging

  • Privacy Assured: Because we handle intimate apparel and loungewear, all Saint Roman orders are shipped in discreet, plain-faced outer packaging without product descriptions or images printed on the exterior shipping label.
  • Tamper-Evident Seals: Every package is secured with custom tamper-evident tape. Do not accept any package if the outer seal is broken, torn, or tampered with at the time of delivery.

7. Delivery attempts & return to origin (RTO)

  • Attempt Limit: Our 3PL courier partners will make a maximum of 3 delivery attempts to reach you.
  • Failed Deliveries (RTO): If delivery fails after 3 attempts due to an incorrect address, unreachable mobile number, or refusal to accept, the package will be designated as Return to Origin (RTO) and sent back to our fulfillment center.
  • Re-dispatching Fee: Re-dispatching an RTO shipment will incur a fresh shipping fee of ₹99 for prepaid orders.

8. Address modification & cancellations

  • Address Updates: You may update your shipping address within 2 hours of order placement by contacting our support team. Once an order status moves to "Dispatched," no address modifications can be made.
  • Refused COD Shipments: Customers who repeatedly refuse delivery of COD orders at their doorstep reserve the risk of having Cash on Delivery privileges permanently disabled on their accounts.

9. Shipment damage, loss, or non-delivery

If your tracking status shows "Delivered" but you have not received your parcel:

  • You must report the non-receipt to our customer service within 48 hours of the delivery timestamp.
  • In the rare event of a parcel lost in transit by our courier partner, Saint Roman will arrange a 100% free replacement dispatch or a full refund.

10. Logistics support & queries

For any delivery delays or shipping queries, reach out to our team:

  • Live Order Tracking: www.saintromanfashion.com/track-order
  • Logistics Desk E-mail: shipping@saintromanfashion.com / support@saintromanfashion.com
  • Customer Support Line: +91-[Insert Customer Support Number]
  • Support Timings: Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST
